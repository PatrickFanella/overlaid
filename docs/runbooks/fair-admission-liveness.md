# Fair admission caller liveness

Tracking: [#117](https://git.subcult.tv/subculture-collective/overlaid/issues/117), part of P2.2 under [#24](https://git.subcult.tv/subculture-collective/overlaid/issues/24), following merged chat/moderation permit turns in PRs #114 and #116.

A waiting action could previously remain the selected tenant turn until its original deadline even after its caller disappeared. With two-minute action deadlines, an idle caller could prevent active work from obtaining an otherwise free permit. Both pacers now use a three-second database-clock eligibility lease for waiting callers through the shared `permitturns` codec.

Canonical registration sets `active_until` to the earlier of database now plus three seconds and the original deadline. A retry refreshes that field without changing the action identity, resource binding, ticket, tenant turn or deadline. Selection ignores a waiter whose eligibility lease has expired. Fair denials already return a poll hint no later than one second; a caller that retries refreshes its eligibility before selection. Slow or disconnected callers may temporarily become inactive and can rejoin with their existing ticket and turn when they retry before the original deadline.

The eligibility lease reserves no budget, channel spacing or provider permit. It does not change the separate thirty-second in-flight fences, provider cooldowns, conservative local attempt deadline, detached completion or one-use action ledger. A granted marker remains protected through the original deadline even when its caller eligibility expires. Pre-claim cleanup retains charged capacity and tenant turns. Original action expiry still forbids registration and cannot be renewed through a heartbeat.

The retained state writer now uses version two. The reader accepts versions one and two, preserving existing hashes, field names, counters, tickets and granted markers. Version-one waiting entries have no eligibility lease and remain inactive until a fresh canonical registration upgrades them. Version-one granted entries remain granted and cannot regain a permit. Unknown versions, lease times after the original deadline and incompatible version-one lease fields fail decoding. Inspection remains read-only and exposes no caller lease or action data.

No SQL migration or role grant changes are required. Full-size version-two fixtures remain within chat's 128 KiB and moderation's 256 KiB database envelopes. Existing action, tenant, sender, channel and client bounds remain. Inactive waiting entries stay retained until their original deadline for identity conflict checks; they can still occupy bounded capacity. This change improves selection liveness, not unlimited admission under hostile load.

Stop older explicit clients before transition. Version-one readers reject version-two rows; a downgrade must stop fair workers and preserve those rows and all budget/claim state. Restore a version-two-capable build to resume admission. Do not clear or rewrite fair state to make an older binary accept it, and do not reset uncertain or claimed actions. No production worker or pool is activated by this change.

The bound uses the database clock, not elapsed wall time. Clock regression, resource cooldowns, in-flight permits, unavailable storage or worker/provider preparation can delay admission independently. Three seconds is an eligibility lease rather than a total delivery-latency guarantee. No continuously active tenant is promised service beyond its original deadline.

Pure tests cover expiry at the exact lease boundary, active-caller progress, rejoining without a new ticket/turn/deadline, deadline-clamped leases, granted-marker protection, malformed bounds, legacy reads/upgrades and maximum retained shapes. Disposable PostgreSQL tests for both chat and moderation expire only the missing caller's eligibility, prove another tenant can obtain the resource while the first action deadline is still future, then verify the original caller rejoins with unchanged identity. Synthetic clock edits isolate this behavior; they do not measure a natural timeout or live provider throughput. Run `GOTOOLCHAIN=go1.26.0 bash scripts/ci-postgres.sh` for make verify and both uncached race suites. Fixtures send no live provider messages.
